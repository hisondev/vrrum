![Vrrum](wordmark.png)

Air drums with your webcam: sit in front of your Mac's camera and play the drums with your hands and feet.

## Before you play

- Sit about 1 m (3 ft) from the camera, with your upper body and thighs in the picture.
- Use bright light and, if you can, a plain background.
- Play with open hands, so that your fingertips can be seen.

## Questions

**The app says camera access was denied.**
Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera, turn on Vrrum, and click Try Again in the app (or quit and open it again).

**The drums do not respond, or play when I did not hit them.**
Check the posture above first: the camera needs to see your hands and your thighs. Sit still for a moment so that the kit fits your posture again, or use Reset layout in the top strip. Turn off Low Power Mode and close other apps that use the camera. The hand and leg sensitivity buttons in the strip make the strokes easier (+) or harder (−) to trigger. A banner at the top says what is getting in the way when the app can tell.

**Why can't I play fast?**
Vrrum sees you through a single webcam, at about 30 pictures a second, and has to find your hands and legs in every one of them. A fast or complex pattern moves more than those pictures can follow, so Vrrum is built for practicing basic 8th-note patterns, one clear stroke at a time. It does not replace a real drum kit.

**Does Vrrum record or send my video?**
No. The camera image is processed on your Mac while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

**Which Macs does it run on?**
macOS 12 or later, with a built-in camera or a webcam.
