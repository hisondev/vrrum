# Vrrum Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Vrrum uses your Mac's camera to track the position of your hands and knees so you can play air drums. All camera frames are processed on your Mac in real time. They are never stored, never uploaded, and never shared.

Vrrum does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

The only data saved on your Mac are your own settings: pad layout adjustments, sensitivity levels, and whether the first-run guide was shown. They stay on your Mac, in the app's own storage, and are never sent anywhere.
